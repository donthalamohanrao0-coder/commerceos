# NovaTech Brand & Conversation Guidelines

## Voice
Be concise, useful, and human. Avoid exaggerated marketing language.

### Good
"Option A is the best fit if battery life matters more than display quality."

### Bad
"This AMAZING product is definitely the BEST choice for everyone!"

## Recommendation formula
1. Restate the requirement briefly.
2. Present 2–4 relevant options.
3. Explain the strongest match.
4. Mention important trade-offs.
5. Offer a clear next action.

## Upsell formula
- Explain why the accessory complements the selected product.
- State the price.
- Let the customer decline without friction.

## Never
- pressure the customer
- invent reviews
- invent inventory
- fabricate product specifications
- claim a discount that the backend has not validated

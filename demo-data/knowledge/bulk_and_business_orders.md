# Bulk & Business Orders

NovaTech runs a dedicated business desk for organisations buying laptops,
displays and accessories at volume. This document covers eligibility, pricing,
invoicing, delivery and cancellation for orders placed through that channel.

## Who qualifies

A bulk order is any single order for **10 units or more** of the same product, or
a mixed cart with **25 units or more** in total. Registered businesses, schools,
NGOs and government departments are eligible. Individuals cannot place bulk
orders. You must provide a valid GSTIN (or a government exemption certificate) at
checkout for the business price to apply.

## Volume pricing tiers

Discounts are applied to the pre-tax line total and stack on top of any active
campaign, capped so the combined discount never exceeds 20%.

- **10–24 units:** 5% off
- **25–49 units:** 8% off
- **50–99 units:** 12% off
- **100+ units:** priced by the business desk, typically 15–18% off

Quotes are valid for 14 days. A quote does not reserve stock; stock is confirmed
only when the order is placed and paid for (or approved for invoicing).

## Payment and invoicing

Orders under ₹5,00,000 are prepaid by card, UPI or bank transfer. Orders of
₹5,00,000 or more can request **net-30 invoicing** after a one-time credit check,
which takes 2–3 business days. Net-30 accounts get a named account manager and a
priority support line. A GST tax invoice is issued for every business order within
24 hours of dispatch; it is emailed to the billing contact and available in the
account portal.

## Delivery

Bulk orders ship in **5–9 business days** because units are quality-checked and
asset-tagged before dispatch. Express delivery is not available for bulk orders.
For 50+ units NovaTech arranges a single palletised shipment with a delivery
appointment; partial deliveries can be requested at the time of ordering.
Asset tags and a device serial manifest (CSV) are included with every shipment.

## Changes and cancellation

- Before dispatch: a confirmed bulk order can be cancelled or reduced in quantity
  at no charge. Increases are treated as a new order at the current tier.
- After dispatch: cancellation incurs a **15% restocking fee** and the customer
  pays return freight. Units must be unopened and in resale condition.
- Faulty units are replaced under the standard warranty and do not count toward
  the restocking fee.

## Support and rollouts

Net-30 business accounts can request a staged rollout plan, BIOS/firmware
standardisation and a shared spares pool (bill-on-use). Imaging services (a
custom OS image applied before dispatch) are available for orders of 25+
identical units for a flat ₹450 per unit.

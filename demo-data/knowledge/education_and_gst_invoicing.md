# Education Pricing & GST Invoicing

This guide answers common questions about the student/education discount and about
GST invoices for individuals and businesses.

## Education pricing

NovaTech offers an education discount to verified students, teachers and staff of
recognised institutions.

Q: How much is the education discount?
A: 8% off laptops and displays, and 5% off accessories. It is capped at ₹8,000
   off per order and can be used up to twice per academic year per person.

Q: Who is eligible?
A: Currently enrolled students (any level), full-time teaching staff, and
   administrative staff of a recognised school, college or university in India.

Q: How does verification work?
A: You verify once through the education-verification partner using your
   institution email or an ID document. Verification lasts 12 months and then
   needs renewal. Approval is usually instant and at most 2 business days.

Q: Can the education discount be combined with other offers?
A: It can be combined with a trade-in, but not with another storewide campaign —
   the larger of the two is applied. It cannot be combined with bulk/business
   pricing.

Q: Does it apply to gift cards or already-discounted clearance items?
A: No. Gift cards, clearance and open-box items are excluded.

## GST invoicing

Q: Do I get a GST invoice as an individual?
A: Yes. Every order gets a GST tax invoice with NovaTech's GSTIN and the tax
   breakdown. It is emailed after dispatch and downloadable from Orders.

Q: How do I get the invoice in my company's name with input tax credit?
A: Enter your company's legal name, billing address and **GSTIN** in the "Business
   invoice" section at checkout before paying. The invoice is then raised to that
   GSTIN so your business can claim input tax credit. This cannot be changed after
   the invoice is generated.

Q: I forgot to add my GSTIN — can you re-issue the invoice?
A: Only within 7 days of the invoice date and only if the order has not shipped.
   Contact support with the order number and GSTIN. After 7 days or after
   dispatch the invoice is final.

Q: Is GST charged on the amount after discounts?
A: GST is charged on the taxable value, which is the price after campaign and
   education discounts but **before** any trade-in credit or gift-card
   redemption. Shipping charges, when they apply, are taxed at the same rate as
   the main item.

Q: What tax rate applies?
A: Laptops, tablets and most accessories are taxed at 18%. The exact rate for
   each line is shown on the invoice.

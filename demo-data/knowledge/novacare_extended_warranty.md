# NovaCare Extended Warranty

Every NovaTech product includes a standard manufacturer warranty (1 year for
accessories, 2 years for laptops and displays). **NovaCare** is an optional paid
plan that extends and broadens that cover. This document describes the plans,
what is and isn't covered, and how to make a claim.

## Plans and price

NovaCare must be purchased within **45 days** of the device purchase and covers
from the original purchase date.

- **NovaCare Essential** — extends the manufacturer warranty to a total of 3
  years. Covers manufacturing defects and component failure. Priced at 6% of the
  device price.
- **NovaCare Plus** — everything in Essential, plus **accidental damage**
  (drops, spills, cracked screens) with up to two accepted incidents per year,
  each subject to a service fee (₹1,500 for accessories, ₹3,500 for laptops,
  ₹4,500 for displays). Priced at 11% of the device price.
- **NovaCare Complete** — everything in Plus, plus one **battery replacement**
  when health falls below 80%, and **next-business-day onsite service** in
  serviceable PIN codes. Priced at 16% of the device price.

## What is covered

- Manufacturing defects and failure of internal components (motherboard, RAM,
  storage, keyboard, trackpad, panel, ports).
- Screen and hinge failure not caused by the customer (Essential and above).
- Accidental damage from handling — drops, liquid spills, cracked screens (Plus
  and above, with the service fee).
- Battery replacement once during the term when measured health is below 80%
  (Complete only).

## What is not covered

- Loss or theft. NovaCare is not insurance.
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function (scuffs, dents, worn keycaps).
- Damage from unauthorised repair, modification, or non-NovaTech parts.
- Consumables and wear items: styluses, ear tips, palm rests, carry cases.
- Software issues, data loss, and reinstallation of the operating system.
- Deliberate damage or damage from commercial hire/kiosk use.

## Making a claim

1. Start a claim in the account portal or by contacting support with the device
   serial number.
2. For defects, ship the device with the prepaid label; for onsite plans a
   technician is scheduled.
3. Turnaround is typically **3–5 business days** for depot repair, next business
   day for Complete onsite. A loaner can be requested for laptop depot repairs.
4. **Back up your data first.** Repairs may wipe the device, and NovaTech is not
   responsible for data on a device sent for service.

## Transfers and refunds

NovaCare can be transferred once to a new owner if the device is sold; update the
owner in the portal. Cancelling within 30 days of buying NovaCare is a full
refund if no claim has been made; after 30 days it is a pro-rata refund minus the
value of any claims paid.

# Trade-In Program

NovaTech accepts an eligible used laptop, tablet or phone as partial payment
toward a new purchase. This guide explains eligibility, how the value is decided,
and how the credit is applied.

## What can be traded in

- Laptops and tablets no more than **6 years old** that power on, hold a charge,
  and are not locked to another account or carrier.
- Phones no more than **5 years old** in the same condition.
- The device must be factory reset with activation lock / "Find My" disabled and
  no passcode.
- Devices that are cracked, water-damaged, swollen, or missing parts are still
  accepted but graded lower; devices that do not power on have **recycle-only**
  value (no trade credit) and can be dropped off free.

## How the value is set

You get an **instant online estimate** from the model, age and a short condition
questionnaire. The final value is confirmed after inspection at the warehouse and
can differ from the estimate. Grades:

- **Excellent** — like new, no marks, full battery health. 100% of the estimate.
- **Good** — light wear, battery health 80%+. ~80% of the estimate.
- **Fair** — visible wear or battery health below 80%. ~55% of the estimate.
- **Damaged** — cracks, dead pixels, dents affecting use. ~25% of the estimate.

If the confirmed value is lower than the estimate you can accept the revised
offer or have the device returned free within 14 days.

## How the credit is applied

Trade-in credit is applied to the order at checkout as a discount line; it cannot
exceed the order total and is not paid out as cash. If you trade in after buying,
the credit is issued as store credit valid for 12 months. Trade-in credit does
not earn or count toward campaign minimums and is applied after any campaign
discount. GST is charged on the full pre-credit price.

## The process

1. Get the estimate online and add the trade-in to your new-device order.
2. A prepaid box or label arrives within 3 business days. Send the old device
   within **14 days** or the trade-in is cancelled.
3. Inspection takes 3–5 business days. You are emailed the confirmed value.
4. On acceptance, the discount is settled — refunded to your original payment
   method if you had paid the full amount, or applied as store credit.

## Data

NovaTech performs a certified data wipe on every traded-in device and issues a
wipe certificate on request. Still, **you should wipe the device yourself and
remove all accounts before sending it** — NovaTech is not responsible for data on
a device in transit.

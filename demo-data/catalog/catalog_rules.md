# Catalog Rules

- Product IDs and SKUs are immutable identifiers.
- Product price comes from the database.
- Product stock comes from the inventory service.
- Product descriptions are merchant-controlled content.
- Reviews are supporting evidence, not authoritative product specifications.
- AI must not infer missing specifications as facts.
- A product can be recommended only if it is compatible with the stated use case and available/eligible according to current commerce state.

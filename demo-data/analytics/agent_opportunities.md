# AI Growth Opportunities

## Opportunity 1 — Laptop accessory attach rate
Evidence:
- Laptop customers frequently purchase mice and hubs.
- Current observed attach rate is below the merchant's target.
Action:
- Recommend a transparent accessory bundle.
Guardrail:
- No fake popularity claims.

## Opportunity 2 — Developer segment
Evidence:
- Developer customers show higher AOV on 16GB+ laptops.
Action:
- Improve recommendation quality for coding queries.
Guardrail:
- Do not oversell 32GB if 16GB satisfies the stated workload.

## Opportunity 3 — Audio upsell
Evidence:
- Audio buyers often add a mouse only when they also have a laptop purchase.
Action:
- Keep audio upsells focused on relevant accessories rather than unrelated products.

# Campaign Playbook

## Objective
Increase revenue without deceptive or aggressive sales behavior.

## Safe opportunities
- Complementary accessories
- Relevant bundles
- Segment-specific offers
- Cart recovery
- Product education

## Campaign recommendation evidence
A campaign recommendation must include:
- target segment
- eligible products
- observed baseline
- expected benefit range
- reason
- policy constraints

## Prohibited
- fake countdowns
- fake stock warnings
- fabricated social proof
- hidden discounts
- discounts that exceed merchant policy

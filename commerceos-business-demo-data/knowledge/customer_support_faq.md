# Customer Support FAQ
Q: How do I track an order?
A: Open Orders and select the order. Tracking becomes available after carrier handoff.

Q: Can I change an order after payment?
A: Contact support as soon as possible. Changes are subject to fulfillment status.

Q: How do I request a return?
A: Open the eligible order and choose Return Request.

Q: What if an item arrived damaged?
A: Report it within 48 hours with photos where possible.

# Payment FAQ
Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: Razorpay Cards, UPI, Netbanking and supported wallets.

Q: Was I charged if the payment screen failed?
A: The system checks the authoritative payment state before retrying. Customers should not submit a second payment merely because the browser timed out.

Q: Can the AI pay automatically?
A: The agent prepares and requests payment, but customer confirmation is required before payment initiation under the current policy.

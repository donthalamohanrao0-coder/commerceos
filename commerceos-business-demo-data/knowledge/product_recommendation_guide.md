# Product Recommendation Guide
For coding:
- prioritize RAM, storage, processor class, thermals, battery and portability.
- 16GB RAM is the default recommendation for serious development.
- 32GB is preferred for heavier workloads.

For students:
- prioritize value, battery, weight and warranty.

For travel:
- prioritize weight, battery and charging convenience.

For desk setups:
- complementary accessories can include mouse, keyboard, hub and laptop stand.
